STAGE 00 // PARTICIPANT REGISTRATION

Enter the cyber defence simulation.

Welcome to the Human Firewall cybersecurity awareness programme. You will complete an initial assessment, participate in three training modules, investigate workplace security incidents, and complete two further assessments.

Participant information

This project evaluates whether gamified cybersecurity training can improve cybersecurity awareness among non-technical employees.

Your participation involves answering cybersecurity questions and completing simulated workplace security activities.

A randomly generated participant code will be used to associate your assessment responses. Your name is not required for this training session.

Assessment responses and results will be stored for academic evaluation. Results will be analysed for research purposes.

Participation is voluntary. You may leave the session at any time. Leaving does not erase responses already submitted.

Do not enter real passwords, confidential workplace information, or personal account credentials anywhere in this simulation.

TRAINING SIMULATION ONLY. All accounts, messages, URLs and incidents shown in this platform are fictional.

Your participant code will be generated when you register.
